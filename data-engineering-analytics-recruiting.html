Data Engineering & Analytics Recruiting

The hiring challenge

Find the people behind reliable data.

A data engineer building production pipelines, an analytics engineer creating trusted business models and a scientist developing predictive methods can all work with data—yet their skills, responsibilities and measures of success differ.

A productive search begins with what the team needs: trustworthy reporting, scalable data movement, better data products, more mature governance or stronger foundations for AI and machine learning.

That context guides the technical search and helps distinguish hands-on capability from a resume full of platforms and buzzwords.

Specialized recruiting

Data talent from foundations to decisions.

Data Engineers

Engineers who build and operate ingestion, transformation, orchestration and data pipelines, with attention to performance, quality and maintainability.

Analytics Engineers

Specialists who model business data, define reliable metrics, test transformations and bridge data infrastructure with usable analysis.

Data Platform & Architecture

Builders and architects of warehouses, lakehouses, streaming systems and shared data services, balancing scale, access, cost and reliability.

Data Scientists & Applied Analytics

Professionals who frame problems, analyze complex data, develop models and turn evidence into decisions or product capabilities.

Business Intelligence & Analytics

Engineers and analysts who build reporting, dashboards, semantic models and measurement systems used by business and product teams.

Data Leadership

Heads of Data, data engineering managers and senior technical leaders who set priorities, guide architecture and build capable teams.

Define the real mandate

What does your data team need to make possible?

Build or modernize?

Does the team need its first dependable pipeline, a new analytics stack, migration from legacy systems or an established platform that runs at scale?

Data quality & ownership

Who is accountable for lineage, testing, observability, definitions and the trustworthiness of the data used in decisions?

Technical environment

Batch or streaming? Warehouse or lakehouse? Cloud-native or hybrid? The architecture and operating constraints shape the right experience.

Business & AI outcomes

Will the new hire enable self-service analytics, improve decision-making or supply the governed, accessible data needed for AI products?

The BullsEye! approach

Search built around technical relevance.

01

Understand the data challenge

Clarify the systems, stakeholders, engineering constraints and measurable outcomes the new hire must own.

02

Map the relevant market

Identify professionals whose experience matches the actual data work—not simply the same tools listed in a job description.

03

Look at data systems built, reliability improved, complexity handled, stakeholder collaboration and the scope of technical decisions.

04
