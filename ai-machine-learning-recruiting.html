The hiring challenge

Find the person who can actually deliver.

“AI engineer” can mean very different things: designing retrieval systems, building LLM-enabled applications, training models, improving inference, developing evaluation pipelines or running ML infrastructure in production.

The right hire depends on the problem, the architecture and the team—not a fashionable job title. We begin with what the business needs the person to achieve, then define the technical evidence that matters.

A sharper search brief

What we clarify before searching.

Prototype or production?

Does success mean rapid experimentation, customer-facing product delivery, large-scale deployment or improving an existing AI system?

What must the person own?

We separate hands-on engineering, research, platform work, architecture and leadership rather than combining them into an unrealistic wish list.

What is the technical environment?

Software architecture, data maturity, cloud infrastructure, security requirements and product constraints all shape the candidate profile.

What evidence matters?

We look for relevant work, decision-making responsibility, delivery context and the ability to contribute in your environment—not just tool names.
