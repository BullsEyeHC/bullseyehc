The mandate comes first

Hire for the next chapter. Not the last job title.

A CTO for a founder-led company is not necessarily the CTO who should lead a mature engineering organization. A Head of AI needs more than model fluency. And a VP of Engineering must be able to lead people and delivery—not simply have been a strong engineer.

Before the search begins, BullsEye! works to understand the decisions the leader will own, the organization they will inherit or build, and what success must look like after the hire.

Technical leadership recruiting

Critical roles. High-consequence decisions.

Chief Technology Officer

Technical strategy, architecture, executive leadership and the ability to connect technology decisions to the company's business priorities.

Chief Information Officer

Enterprise technology leadership, information systems, modernization, governance and the coordination of technology with operating needs.

VP of Engineering

Engineering execution, organizational design, delivery discipline, management depth and the development of teams that can scale.

Head of AI / AI Leadership

Leaders who can connect AI opportunity to practical architecture, delivery teams, evaluation, risk and measurable business outcomes.

Head of Data / Data Leadership

Executives and senior leaders responsible for data strategy, platforms, quality, analytics and the foundations of AI-enabled products.

Engineering Directors & Technical Product Leaders

Leaders accountable for engineering organizations, complex products, cross-functional decisions and translating strategy into execution.

What separates a fit from a familiar title

Leadership search requires context.

Company stage & ownership

Does the leader need to build from zero, stabilize a team, integrate acquisitions, scale a product or transform an established organization?

Technical judgment

Which architecture, security, platform, AI or product decisions will the leader make—and how deep must their direct technical experience be?

People & organizational leadership

What size and type of team will they lead? What operating rhythm, management capability and talent-development judgment are essential?

Executive partnership

How must this person work with the CEO, board, product leadership, customers and commercial stakeholders? Where does decision authority sit?

AI is a major leadership priority. The search may require a technical executive who understands applied AI, LLM products, ML infrastructure and the surrounding engineering organization—but the right brief depends on what the company is actually building.

The BullsEye! approach

A search process built around judgment.

01

Define the executive mandate

Clarify business outcomes, decision rights, reporting relationships, technical requirements and the reasons the role matters now.

02

Map the relevant leadership market

Identify executives and emerging leaders whose experience aligns with the company's actual stage, complexity and direction.

03

Assess leadership fit

Look beyond credentials to the work they have owned, teams they have led, decisions they have made and conditions in which they perform.

04

Guide the search through close

Coordinate candidate conversations, interviews, feedback and offer-stage decisions with attention to the mandate and mutual fit.
