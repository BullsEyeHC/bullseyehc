OT Security Recruiting

Protecting industrial operations requires a different kind of security experience. Operational Technology (OT) environments combine industrial control systems, connected equipment, production processes and safety-critical constraints. BullsEye! has worked on specialized OT security recruiting assignments and helps organizations identify professionals who understand that reality—not just enterprise IT security.

A distinct hiring discipline

OT security is not simply IT security on a factory floor.

In industrial and critical infrastructure settings, security decisions can affect uptime, equipment, safety and physical processes. Maintenance windows may be limited, systems may have long lifecycles, and a security control that works well in corporate IT may be unsuitable for production operations.

Finding the right person means distinguishing candidates who understand industrial environments, ICS/SCADA architecture and operational constraints from those whose background is predominantly conventional IT cybersecurity.

Why the mandate matters

Hire for the plant, process and risk—not just the job title.

Which operational environment?

Manufacturing, energy, utilities and other industrial settings can require different familiarity with equipment, control systems and safety expectations.

Which systems are in scope?

PLCs, DCS, SCADA, HMIs, historians, engineering workstations and industrial networks create distinct architecture and security challenges.

What security responsibility?

Asset discovery, segmentation, secure remote access, vulnerability management, architecture, governance and detection are not interchangeable disciplines.

How will operations be protected?

We look for evidence of judgment about uptime, change control, vendor dependencies, incident planning and coordination with operational stakeholders.
