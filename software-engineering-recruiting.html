Why these hires matter

Get beyond the stack. Find the capability.

Two people can list the same languages and frameworks on a resume while having very different abilities to design systems, debug production failures, make tradeoffs and deliver with a team.

The strongest hiring brief starts with the problems an engineer must solve: building a new product, modernizing a platform, improving performance, taking ownership of a service or raising the technical bar across an organization.

We connect that context to an evidence-based search for the people most likely to succeed in your environment.

Define the real mandate

The right engineer depends on the work.

New build or existing system?

Greenfield product development calls for different strengths than modernization, migration, scale, or keeping critical systems dependable.

Depth or breadth?

Does the role need deep expertise in one technical domain, broad ownership across the stack, or leadership without a management title?

Team and environment fit

How are decisions made? What technical support exists? What is the pace of delivery, and what level of ambiguity must the engineer handle?

Evidence of actual impact

We look for the scope of work owned, tradeoffs made, systems improved and contribution to outcomes—not only technologies listed on a resume.
