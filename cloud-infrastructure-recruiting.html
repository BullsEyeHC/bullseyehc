Cloud & Infrastructure Recruiting

The systems behind the product are part of the product. When your infrastructure has to scale, stay secure and support engineering delivery, hiring the right technical people becomes a business-critical decision. BullsEye! helps companies recruit cloud, platform, DevOps, site reliability and infrastructure engineering talent matched to the work—not just the tools on a resume.

Beyond certifications

Recruit for the systems they will own.

“Cloud engineer” can describe someone who provisions environments, designs distributed architectures, builds an internal developer platform, manages reliability or leads a major modernization. Those are different jobs with different evidence of success.

We begin with the operating environment, technical challenges, ownership and business impact of the role. That context helps identify people who can make sound tradeoffs under the conditions your team actually faces.

Specialized technical recruiting

Cloud, platform & reliability talent.

Cloud Engineers & Architects

People who design, build and evolve cloud environments, balancing performance, resilience, security, cost and practical operations.

DevOps & Automation Engineers

Talent focused on infrastructure as code, delivery pipelines, automation and improving how applications reach production.

Site Reliability Engineers

Engineers who understand service-level objectives, observability, incidents, capacity, operational risk and the reliability of production systems.

Platform Engineering

Builders of internal platforms and developer infrastructure that help software teams deliver with greater speed, consistency and control.

Distributed Systems & Infrastructure

Specialists working on scalability, networking, systems performance, data movement and the architecture behind complex applications.

Infrastructure Technical Leaders

Engineering managers, directors and senior technical contributors who can establish priorities, lead teams and own critical infrastructure decisions.

Get the search brief right

What does reliable execution require?

Build, operate or modernize?

Does the hire need to create infrastructure from scratch, improve a running platform, migrate systems or stabilize an environment under pressure?

Scale & criticality

We distinguish experience with complex, high-availability systems from familiarity with similar tools in a much simpler environment.

Engineering ownership

Will the person write production code, lead architecture, develop automation, carry operational responsibility or mentor other engineers?

Security, cost & constraints

Regulatory needs, access controls, cost management, incident risk and existing architecture can change what an effective hire looks like.
